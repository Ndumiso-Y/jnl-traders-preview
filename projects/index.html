Projects & Our Work

Work Deliveredon Mines andSmelters

Since 2016 JNL Traders has repaired, fabricated and maintained equipment and structures for mining shafts, smelters, refineries and a platinum mine.

Types of Work

What the project record covers

Grouped from the completed-projects record in the JNL Traders company profile.

  • Underground rolling stock

    Manufacture and repair of guard cars, buffers, drill cages and rail-mounted explosives transport cars for mining shafts.

  • Hoppers, silos and chutes

    Repair of wide-door hoppers, silo structural repairs, and fabrication of silo shutter rings and underground transfer chutes.

  • Fabrication and installation

    Fabrication on-site and off-site for smelter and refinery operations, running from 2022 to the present.

  • Site maintenance

    Ongoing on-site maintenance at smelter and refinery sites since 2022.
