Sectors We Serve

Where our work is done

The environments in the JNL Traders project record, and the kind of work each one has called for.

  • Mining shafts

    Repair of guard cars, buffers, drill cages and transport cars; wide-door hoppers and silo structural repairs.

  • Smelters

    Furnace maintenance, shutdown support, fabrication and installation, and ongoing site maintenance.

  • Refineries and processing plants

    Thickener repairs and maintenance, pumps and pipework, and fabrication and site maintenance.

  • Platinum operations

    Fabrication of silo cutter rings and underground transfer chutes.

Partners & Suppliers

How we work with others

JNL Traders selects suppliers and subcontractors for the right fit with the work and with the business, and maintains professionalism, integrity, honesty and fairness in each of those relationships.

We believe in skills transfer, both among our own employees and to newly established local companies, so that the capacity we build stays in the area.

  • Suppliers and subcontractors selected for fit and quality
  • Skills transfer to newly established local companies
  • Quality documentation shared with clients as the work progresses
