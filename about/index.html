Who We Are

A local engineering fabrication company serving mining and processing operations

JNL Traders was registered in 2013 as an engineering fabrication company and began operating on the mines in 2015. The company comes from the Bafokeng area near Rustenburg and has grown around the structural, mechanical and plant maintenance needs of mining and smelter operations.

The work is carried out by qualified, skilled personnel, and skills transfer matters to us: to our own employees, and to newly established local companies that work alongside us.

Our aim is to give each client a dependable, complete solution from one team, and to keep earning the reputation we have built through consistent service.

How We Work

Quality you can document

JNL Traders runs an in-house quality system built around an integrated, complete quality-control process. Every project is planned, recorded and handed over with its paperwork.

  1. 01Planning and method statementsProject progress planning and scheduling, with method statements, work instructions, checklists and quality plans per project and customer
  2. 02Quality control plansUp-to-date QCPs for fabrication and erection
  3. 03As-built data packsAs-built documentation and data packs created as the work progresses
  4. 04Proof of qualityStandard-setting and testing of the items used, with proof-of-quality documentation supplied to clients on an ongoing basis

Our Approach to Business

Project-centred, tailored to each requirement

JNL Traders is an engineering project-centred company. We tailor solutions and services to each customer’s business and project requirements rather than offering a fixed package.

Our background is in fabrication, construction and erection, with an emphasis on commercial and industrial steel construction carried out in a safe and cost-effective manner.

We take a holistic view of what a customer needs and keep improving how we plan, implement, manage and support projects, so that clients see the benefit in greater cost efficiency.
