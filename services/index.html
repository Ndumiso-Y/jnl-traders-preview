Core Service 01

Structural Services

Fabrication, construction and erection are where JNL Traders started. We carry out steel fabrication and structural work for industrial and commercial clients, with the emphasis on safe, cost-effective delivery.

  • Draughting and design
  • Steel fabrication
  • Erection and dismantling
  • Structural repairs and maintenance
  • Hoppers, chutes and silo structural repairs
  • Site maintenance of structures

Core Service 02

Mechanical Services

Mechanical repair and maintenance on site, backed by the supply of mechanical components and instrumentation for plant and equipment.

  • Mechanical repairs and maintenance on site
  • Routine conveyor maintenance (see below)
  • Supply of pumps, valves and motors
  • Supply of bearings, V-belts and hydraulic equipment
  • Supply of bolts, nuts, fasteners and pressure gauges
  • Instrumentation: actuators, level-gauge instruments, solenoids and limit-switch boxes

Core Service 03

Plant Operations

Shutdown and maintenance support inside operating plants: furnace maintenance, thickener repairs, and the fabrication and site maintenance that keeps a plant running between shutdowns.

  • Shutdown plant services
  • Furnace maintenance
  • Thickener repairs and maintenance
  • Fabrication and installation on site
  • Ongoing site maintenance

Mechanical Services

Routine conveyor maintenance

Equipment that moves bulk material runs best when it is clean and well maintained. Under this programme our technicians come to site at regular intervals to inspect and attend to the conveyor system.

  • Routine cleaning at the start and end of each day
  • Inspection of conveyor drive components and mechanisms
  • Spillage control
  • Inspection for misalignment
  • Inspection of joints for peeling or damage
  • Emergency-stop functionality checks
  • Spare-part replacement
  • Sweeping and vamping, and supply of conveyor belt structure
